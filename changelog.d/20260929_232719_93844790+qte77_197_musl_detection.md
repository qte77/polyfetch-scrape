### Fixed

- `polyfetch doctor` and the browser (patchright) tier now detect musl libc (Alpine) and fail
  loudly with an actionable message (use a glibc image, or cap escalation with
  `--max-tier curl_cffi` / `max_tier="curl_cffi"`) instead of a cryptic import/launch error or
  a silent failure. Detection is local and network-free; the httpx and curl_cffi tiers are
  unaffected. (#197)
