### Added

- Opt-in `utils.robots.is_allowed(url, user_agent="*")`: fetches and parses a site's `/robots.txt` and reports allow/deny via the stdlib `urllib.robotparser` (RFC 9309). Dual-use — `fetch()` and `discover()` never call it automatically. Reuses `discover()`'s escalation-only SSRF guard for the robots.txt fetch itself; a missing/unreachable/unparseable robots.txt allows everything (#147).
