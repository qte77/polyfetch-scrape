### Added

- `fetch()` gains opt-in `pinned_ip`/`pinned_host` kwargs (httpx and curl_cffi tiers
  only; raises `FetchError` on the patchright tier, or when only one of the pair is
  given). `polyfetch_scrape.utils._ssrf.resolve_pinned()` returns the externally-
  verified address a hostname resolved to, instead of only a pass/raise verdict, and
  `guarded_fetch()` checks, pins, and connects to that address for every redirect hop
  before it is fetched — closing the DNS-rebinding TOCTOU and the curl_cffi
  post-hoc-only redirect check for the three call sites already capped at
  `max_tier="curl_cffi"` (`utils/sitemap.py`'s `_fetch_and_parse`, `utils/discovery.py`'s
  `_fetch` and `_fetch_status`). `check_ssrf`/`check_redirect` stay exported and
  behaviourally unchanged (#240).

### Security

- The `easter_hunt` contrib's `hunt()` is not yet migrated to the pinned
  `guarded_fetch` — it can still reach the unpinned patchright tier (no per-hop
  pinning primitive exists there), so an attacker-triggered fingerprint block can
  escalate it past the pinned tiers. Documented as a residual gap in
  `contrib/easter_hunt/orchestrator.py`'s module docstring, pending an owner decision
  on #240.
- `guarded_fetch()` now **fails closed**: for an external seed, a hop whose address
  `resolve_pinned()` cannot verify (hostless URL, unresolvable host) raises
  `FetchError` instead of falling through to an unpinned fetch — previously this fell
  back to an unpinned request, which on curl_cffi meant libcurl's own
  `allow_redirects=True` default auto-following a redirect chain with nothing
  checking where it landed. `check_ssrf`/`resolve_pinned` keep their documented
  fail-open semantics for their own direct callers; an internal seed keeps today's
  unpinned behaviour. `guarded_fetch()` also keeps a defence-in-depth
  `check_redirect()` call on the landed response, so a pin bypass is still caught.
- The curl_cffi tier now verifies, after every pinned request, that curl actually
  connected to the pinned address (`Response.primary_ip`, normalised through
  `ipaddress` so an IPv4-mapped IPv6 form matches its plain-IPv4 pin) — refusing with
  `FetchError` on any mismatch. This catches a `CurlOpt.RESOLVE` miss from a parser
  differential between `urlsplit` (what the guard checked) and curl's own URL/host
  parsing (backslash, userinfo, percent-encoding). The `CurlOpt.RESOLVE` entry itself
  now brackets an IPv6 pinned address (`host:port:[::1]`, curl.se's documented
  format) and IDNA-encodes a unicode pinned host to the ASCII form curl resolves. The
  httpx tier's `Host` header and `sni_hostname` extension are likewise IDNA-encoded.
